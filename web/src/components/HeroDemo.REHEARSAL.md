# HeroDemo rehearsal

Landing hero, built with the stageshow kit (`src/vendor/stageshow/kit`, MIT).

- **Gold copied:** `device-pair` (window top-left off the bottom, phone straddling its right edge); timing borrowed from `payout-flow` and `dark-hero` (Collapse drop, reverse-stagger exit, reset while closed).
- **Story, ~9.5 s loop:** invoice in the inbox, the agent's `pay` call opens, the contract card drops in and its three checks land 420 ms apart, the Held stamp lands, the lock-screen notification drops in, a 3 s hold, then the call, card and notification leave in one reverse stagger and the checks reset unseen.
- **Poster (reduced motion):** the held frame, everything on screen.
- **Changed from the plan:** the owner's in-app Reject tap was dropped. A satellite phone small enough to sit beside the window can't show app text legibly, so the payoff is the notification at true iOS size.
- **Compact (below 560):** no inbox heading, a tighter verdict, the window bled off the right and about square, a 300-wide phone below the verdict straddling the window's foot.
- **Filmed:** wide loop at 1280 dark, compact loop and poster at 390 dark, wide poster light.
- **Remaining film lines, left on purpose:** `uneven inset` on the kit's own window title bar and on the kit Notification's icon (iOS centers the icon vertically); `small text` on the notification's "now" at 390, which keeps true iOS proportion.
- **Not checked:** a real phone with touch, Safari, the loop running for minutes on a low-power device.
